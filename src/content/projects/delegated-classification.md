## Overview

Master's thesis at Saarland University (in progress).

When a company (the *principal*) delegates training of a classifier to an external ML provider (the *agent*), it needs a contract that pays for good results without seeing all of the agent's effort.

## Approach

- Models the incentive contract between principal and agent as a **Stackelberg game**.
- Develops **robust contracts** that still work well under uncertainty about the delegation setting.
